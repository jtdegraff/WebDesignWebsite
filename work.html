Welcome to Stockville

Welcome to Stockville is a short animation I recently made. Given how every character is a stock image,
my main focus was to use tweens in order to
establish a feeling of life for these still images.

Crabclaws

Crabclaws was made within the span of
roughly 90 minutes for my animation midterm.
This one tested my ability to create and use rigs
in Adobe Animate, as well as various tweens.

MotiMote

MotiMote is a humorous "advertisement" I made for a theoretical company, where you can move things with the push of a button and the flick of your wrist! Utilizing shape tweens in order to produce the transforming effect with the remote was a lot of fun.
